Plain wordsName the part, then say what it does here

Interviewers do not reward a list of product names. “We use Ray” is not an answer; “one Ray driver owns every engine, so it can pause them all before a weight update” is. Each section below gives you that second sentence for one part of the machine.

For leadersWhere the libraries stop

vLLM generates and can be paused and updated in place; it does not tag tokens with weight versions or decide staleness. FSDP2 shards the trainer and DCP checkpoints it asynchronously; it does not know about rollouts. Ray starts and calls processes; the buffer, the staleness policy and the failure policy are your code. Sandboxes and weight sync are where most of the engineering, and most of the interview depth, sits.
