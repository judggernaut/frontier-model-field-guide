Plain wordsA kitchen that cooks, tastes and learns

RL training is a kitchen where cooks (the rollout fleet) try recipes, tasters (reward services) score each dish, and a head chef (the trainer) rewrites the recipe book after every round and hands new copies to every cook (weight sync). Every design question is the same kitchen at a different size: how many cooks, how many tasters, how long the slowest dish takes, and how fast you can hand out new recipe books.

The method: ask a few questions that change the kitchen, do the arithmetic, draw the kitchen, then dig into the part the arithmetic says will break.
